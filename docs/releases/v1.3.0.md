## Antigravity joins the notch

- **A new Antigravity circle.** It fills as you use your Gemini 5-hour limit, the same way Claude's ring follows its session.
- **A full card on hover.** Hover the circle for four limits: Gemini session, Gemini weekly, Claude + GPT session and Claude + GPT weekly. Each shows its reset time, and the card also shows your account and plan.
- **It stays on your Mac.** Usage comes straight from the Antigravity app over an encrypted local connection, and nothing is sent anywhere else.
- **To turn it on:** open Antigravity, sign in, then switch Antigravity on in Settings.

## Choose the assistants you use

- **One switch per assistant in Settings.** On: the circle shows in the notch and its usage is read. Off: it leaves the notch and nothing is read.
- **New installs start empty.** The notch shows a **Choose assistants** button that opens Settings.
- **Updating?** The assistants you already had turned on stay on. Assistants you never turned on no longer appear as empty circles.

## Steadier

- **Antigravity closed:** the circle keeps its last reading, dimmed, and updates on its own when you reopen it.
- **Signed out of Antigravity:** the card asks you to sign in instead of passing off an old reading as current.
- **Switching Antigravity accounts:** no false "limit reset" or "limit reached" alerts.
- **Account and plan names** with underscores or asterisks now display exactly as written.
- **Flipping a switch quickly** on and off no longer leaves a stale approval behind.
